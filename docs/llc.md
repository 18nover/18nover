## Research notes (as of Nov 21, 2026)

- **Articles of organization:** Kentucky FastTrack filing is listed at **$40**. Do not file, and do not pay, without Jerry’s explicit confirmation of the legal name, organizer/registered agent details, and the payment step.
- **EIN:** Apply for a free EIN from the IRS after the LLC exists. There is no filing fee for the EIN itself.
- **Annual report:** **$15**, due January 1 through June 30, starting the calendar year **after** the year the LLC is formed.
- **LLET:** A Kentucky Limited Liability Entity Tax may apply depending on the company’s activity and receipts. Treat this as a “ask a tax preparer” item, not as a conclusion that the company does or does not owe it.
